HOW AI DETECTION WORKS

Automated hazard recognition capabilities and limitations.

Android Clear uses an on-device machine-learning model (EfficientDet-Lite0,
running through TensorFlow Lite inside the app's embedded Python runtime) to
identify environmental hazards and obstacles in real time.

Please note that automated detection is probabilistic and may not identify every
obstacle or hazard. Weather conditions, poor lighting, camera lens obstructions,
motion blur, a dirty lens and sudden movements can all reduce detection accuracy.
The model recognises a fixed set of trained classes: vehicles (bicycle, car,
motorcycle, bus, train, truck) and vertical street fixtures (traffic light, fire
hydrant, stop sign, parking meter). It does not understand every object, does not
measure distance in metres, and does not know whether a detected object is
actually in your path.

All processing happens on your device. No frames, images or detections are
uploaded, and the app contains no analytics or advertising SDKs.

The app is designed to assist your environmental awareness, not to replace your
judgement. Always rely on visual verification and personal discretion, and never
use Android Clear as your only source of information about your surroundings.
