SAFETY & EMERGENCY NOTICE

Android Clear is an informational tool, not a life-safety device.

Do not rely on this application for emergency response, navigation in high-risk
environments, or as a replacement for required safety equipment, a white cane, a
guide dog, or your own attention.

The application does not contact emergency services automatically and cannot call
for help. Detection is probabilistic: it can miss real hazards and it can report
hazards that are not there. Low light, rain, a dirty or covered lens, motion and
device performance all reduce accuracy.

Automatic threat capture, when enabled, stores images on your device. You are
responsible for those images and for how you use them.

By proceeding, you acknowledge that you remain fully responsible for your personal
safety and surroundings at all times.
