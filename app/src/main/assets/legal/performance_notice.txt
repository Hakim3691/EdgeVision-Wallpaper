PERFORMANCE & BACKGROUND LIMITS

Real-time continuous analysis requires significant processing power and camera
resources. Running Android Clear for extended periods may increase battery
consumption and warm the device.

Android may pause or restrict background processing when the device enters
battery-saver mode or when memory is low. Android 11 and later also apply
while-in-use restrictions to the camera, and some manufacturers deny camera access
to wallpaper processes entirely.

What the app does about it:
- the camera is released whenever the screen is off or the lock screen is
  showing, and detection resumes when you unlock;
- the live preview and the detection model run at independent rates, so the
  wallpaper stays smooth without running inference on every frame;
- if the camera cannot be opened, the app retries with increasing delays instead
  of failing permanently.

The settings screen shows the live pipeline status, including the camera state
and the last error, so a restriction is visible rather than silent. If the status
reads camera=error, check your manufacturer's battery and background-app
settings for Android Clear, or open the app once and return to the home screen.
