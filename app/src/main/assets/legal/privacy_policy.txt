ANDROID CLEAR — PRIVACY POLICY

Effective date: 7 November 2026
Applies to: Android Clear (com.edgevision.wallpaper), version 1.4.0 and later.

1. INTRODUCTION

Android Clear is a live wallpaper that shows a translucent rear-camera view on
your home screen and runs on-device hazard detection. This policy explains what
the app does with camera frames, what it stores, and what it never does.

By installing and using Android Clear you agree to the practices described here.
If you do not agree, do not use the app.

2. CAMERA PROCESSING

While the wallpaper is visible, the screen is on and the device is unlocked, the
app opens the rear camera and processes frames entirely on your device:

- each frame is converted into a low-resolution preview that is drawn under your
  home screen;
- a 320x320 RGB copy is passed to a bundled TensorFlow Lite model
  (EfficientDet-Lite0) running on the device through an embedded Python runtime;
- the resulting boxes are evaluated on the device to decide whether to pulse the
  border, play a short tone, vibrate, or speak an alert.

Frames exist only in memory. They are not recorded, not written to storage and
not transmitted anywhere. The camera is closed when the wallpaper is hidden, the
screen turns off, or the lock screen is showing.

3. AUTOMATIC THREAT CAPTURE (OFF BY DEFAULT)

Automatic threat capture is an opt-in feature that is off unless you switch it
on. When it is on and a hazard is confirmed, the app saves a short JPEG burst to
Pictures/Android Clear/Threat Captures through Android's MediaStore.

Those images stay on your device, in your own picture storage, until you delete
them. They are never uploaded by the app. You can delete them with any gallery
or file app, and you can switch the feature off at any time.

4. PERMISSIONS AND WHY THEY ARE REQUESTED

- Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): required for the live preview and for
  on-device hazard detection. Without it the wallpaper shows a transparent
  surface and no detection runs.
- Vibration (android.permission.VIBRATE): used for haptic hazard alerts.
- Storage, legacy devices only (android.permission.WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE,
  capped at Android 9 / API 28): needed to save threat captures on older
  Android versions. Android 10 and later use scoped MediaStore access and do not
  request this permission.

Android Clear does not request or use the microphone, location, contacts,
calendar, SMS, call state, activity recognition, body sensors, or accessibility
services. It does not read your photos, documents or other files; its only
storage write is the capture burst you explicitly enable.

5. NETWORK ACTIVITY AND THIRD PARTIES

Android Clear contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking
SDKs, and requires no account. Frames, detections, settings and captures are not
sent to the developer or to any third party. The app declares no network
permission and performs no network requests.

Google Play itself processes installation, update and licence information under
Google's own privacy policy; that processing is outside the app.

6. RETENTION AND DELETION

- Settings live in the app's private storage on your device.
- Threat captures live in your shared picture storage until you delete them.
- Clearing the app's data (Settings > Apps > Android Clear > Storage > Clear
  data) resets all settings and consent choices.
- Uninstalling Android Clear removes its private data. Captured images remain in
  your gallery until you delete them, because they are your files.

7. CHILDREN

Android Clear is a general-purpose assistive utility and is not directed to
children under 13. It collects no personal information from any user.

8. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this policy to reflect changes in the app or in legal
requirements. The effective date above changes when we do, and the current text
ships inside the app under Settings > About & Legal > Privacy Policy.
